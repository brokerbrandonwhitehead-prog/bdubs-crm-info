BDUBS Brokerage CRM

A private, single-user brokerage workspace operated by Brandon Whitehead. It brings property-related contacts, source emails, documents, and follow-up notes together on the owner’s computer.

How Google data is used

  • Read-only Gmail access captures supported inquiry notices and correspondence carrying existing property labels.
  • Property document attachments can be copied into the CRM and saved to the owner’s existing mapped Google Drive folders.
  • Original-email links preserve the source of captured records. Qualification and outreach decisions remain with the owner.

The application does not automatically send email, modify Gmail labels, or move, overwrite, or delete existing Google Drive files.

This website contains public app information only. It does not host the CRM, provide a login, display brokerage records, or accept new users.
